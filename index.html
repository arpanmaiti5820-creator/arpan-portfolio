Project Overview

CollabNotes helps users manage their notes, organize folders, save favorites, manage attachments and restore deleted notes.

How to Use

  1. Register or sign in to your account.
  2. Create notes and add their titles and content.
  3. Organize notes into folders.
  4. Search notes and mark important notes as favorites.
  5. Use Trash to manage deleted notes.
